# Faculty Schedule View - Quick Start Guide

## 📱 Accessing Your Schedule

### Step 1: Open the Schedule View
1. Open your Medical Scheduling Google Sheet
2. Click on the **"Medical Scheduling"** menu at the top
3. Select **"📅 My Schedule"**

### Step 2: View Your Schedule
A modal window will open showing:
- Your name and email at the top
- A countdown timer to your next duty (if you have upcoming duties)
- Cards for each of your scheduled duties

## 🎯 Features Overview

### Countdown Timer
The large countdown timer at the top shows:
- **Time remaining** until your next duty (in HH:MM:SS format)
- **Duty type** (e.g., Emergency, Ward, OPD)
- **Start time and date**
- **Location** where you need to be

The timer updates **every second** automatically - no need to refresh!

### Schedule Cards
Each duty appears as a card showing:
- **Duty Type** - What kind of duty it is
- **Date & Time** - When it starts and ends
- **Location** - Where you need to be
- **Description** - Additional details (if available)

### Color Coding
Cards are color-coded by duty type for quick identification:
- 🔴 **Red**: Emergency/ER duties
- 🟢 **Green**: Ward duties
- 🟡 **Yellow**: OPD/Outpatient duties
- 🟣 **Purple**: Surgery/OT duties
- 🟠 **Orange**: Lectures/Teaching
- 🔵 **Cyan**: Clinic duties

## 📱 Device Compatibility

### Works on ALL Devices
- **Desktop** - Full multi-column layout
- **Tablet** - Optimized 2-column layout
- **Mobile Phone** - Single column, touch-friendly layout

Simply open the schedule on any device - it automatically adapts!

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: Can I see other faculty members' schedules?
**A:** No, you can only view your own schedule. This is for privacy and security.

### Q: How often does the countdown update?
**A:** The countdown updates every second in real-time without refreshing the page.

### Q: What if I have no upcoming duties?
**A:** The page will display "No Upcoming Duties" message instead of being blank.

### Q: What happens when my duty time arrives?
**A:** The countdown automatically switches to your next upcoming duty.

### Q: Can I print my schedule?
**A:** Yes! Use your browser's print function (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P). The layout is optimized for printing.

### Q: Why can't I see the schedule?
**A:** Make sure your email is registered in the Faculty sheet with Active status. Contact your admin if you need to be added.
